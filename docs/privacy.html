Privacy Policy

Pdfzy (Android app, package com.oamsapps.pdfzy) · Developer: Oamprakash R · Effective 18 November 2026

In short: the PDFs, images and scans you work with are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we don't collect your files. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

1. Your files

Every tool (compress, scan, merge, split, extract, rotate, convert, protect and unlock) runs entirely on your device. Files are read from and saved to locations you choose, and are never sent over the internet. Passwords you enter to protect or unlock a PDF are kept in memory only for that task and are never stored.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a list of recent files (file name, size, the tool used, time, and a link to the file on your phone), your ad-free purchase status, and basic preferences such as how many tasks you've completed. This stays on your device. You can remove items from the Recent list at any time, and everything is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

4. Your ad choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying "Remove ads" stops all ads in the app.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: used by Google's document scanner only while you scan, inside the scanner screen.
  • Notifications: to show progress of long tasks and tell you when your file is ready. Optional.
  • Foreground service: keeps a long task (for example compressing a large PDF) running if you switch apps.
  • Internet: only for ads, consent and purchases. Your files never use it.

The app doesn't request access to all your files. You pick each file through Android's own file and photo pickers.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Because we don't hold your files or personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google, use the links above or your Google account settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have further rights; contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
